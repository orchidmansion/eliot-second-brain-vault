# What Your CMO Needs To Know about AI Search - Tom Critchlow + Ross Hudgens

- Tab 1

Here is a comprehensive, section-by-section chronological breakdown of the discussion between Ross Hudgens and Tom Critchlow.

### [00:00:00 - 00:02:46] The Executive Stance on AI

**The "Yes" Philosophy:** Tom stresses that when a CMO or executive asks if the SEO team is using specific tools like Profound or creating AI-generated content, the answer must always be a strategic "yes" [[00:17](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D17&authuser=1)].

**Avoiding the Defensive Trap:** SEO professionals have spent years playing defense against poor executive ideas [[00:32](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D32&authuser=1)]. However, acting defensively when leadership is genuinely excited about a major industry shift (like AI search or GEO) is a career risk that can get you locked out of the room where decisions are made [[00:52](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D52&authuser=1), [01:28](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D88&authuser=1)].

**Framing AI Workflows:** A "yes" doesn't mean you must automate everything end-to-end; it means you frame your response around *how* you are actively evaluating and integrating AI into the content workflow [[01:16](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D76&authuser=1), [01:47](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D107&authuser=1)].

### [00:02:47 - 00:05:59] Launching "AI Search Leaders" & The Measurement Problem

**Evolution of the SEO MBA:** Tom explains his new community, *AI Search Leaders*, which grew out of his work with the SEO MBA [[02:47](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D167&authuser=1)]. The core thesis is that the entire industry is collectively scrambling to learn a brand-new playbook involving shifting user behaviors, tools, and platforms like ChatGPT and Google's AI mode [[03:03](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D183&authuser=1), [03:15](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D195&authuser=1)].

**The Ubiquitous Question:** By far, the most frequent problem senior professionals are trying to solve in the community is **how to measure performance** in an AI environment [[03:52](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D232&authuser=1), [03:57](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D237&authuser=1)].

**The Rise of Prompt Monitoring Upstarts:** Tom notes that newer tools like Profound and Prompt Watch are seeing exponential spending growth (visible via platforms like Ramp) [[04:11](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D251&authuser=1), [04:27](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D267&authuser=1)]. He suggests legacy giants like SEMrush and Ahrefs are facing immense competition as they fight to keep pace with these agile upstarts [[04:50](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D290&authuser=1), [05:00](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D300&authuser=1)].

### [00:06:00 - 00:10:23] Shifting from Keyword Rank to Brand Share

**How vs. Where:** In traditional SEO, rank tracking was a proxy for "share of voice" based on *where* a brand appeared [[05:24](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D324&authuser=1), [05:32](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D332&authuser=1)]. In AI search, the critical metric is *how* you appear [[06:00](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D360&authuser=1)]. Tom highlights a client who appeared as a citation for nearly every search in their category, but the LLM explicitly warned users *not* to buy from them—a qualitative problem that didn't exist in traditional search [[06:08](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D368&authuser=1), [06:25](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D385&authuser=1)].

**The Core Brand Metrics:** GEO measurement aligns far more with traditional brand marketing than classical performance SEO [[06:34](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D394&authuser=1), [09:54](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D594&authuser=1)]. Teams should measure:

Brand awareness, consideration, preference, and qualitative modifiers (e.g., if the LLM thinks you are too expensive or have poor customer service) [[06:41](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D401&authuser=1), [06:57](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D417&authuser=1)].

**Reynolds' Four-Prompt Framework:** For brands wanting to start small, Tom references an open-source framework by Will Reynolds that checks brand perception across simple API prompts [[08:04](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D484&authuser=1), [08:10](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D490&authuser=1)]. This lets you see what the model knows based on training data alone, what it knows with live web access, and who it recommends in a head-to-head comparison against competitors [[08:19](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D499&authuser=1), [08:44](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D524&authuser=1)].

### [00:10:24 - 00:15:37] Down-Funnel Behavior & Self-Attribution Data

**The 10,000-Word Chat:** Ross shares a recent internal analysis showing that "versus" landing pages heavily influence user decisions and traffic [[10:37](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D637&authuser=1)]. Tom agrees, noting that consumers are spending much more of the funnel interacting directly with LLMs before ever clicking through to a brand's site [[11:18](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D678&authuser=1), [11:45](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D705&authuser=1)]. Real buying decisions aren't single-query events; they involve long, conversational back-and-forths [[12:07](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D727&authuser=1)].

**Synthetic User Personas:** Mentioning conversations with Mike King of iPullRank, Tom notes that the future of testing may rely on simulated, multi-turn conversational testing rather than basic single-prompt tracking [[12:49](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D769&authuser=1), [13:02](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D782&authuser=1)].

**The 40% Lead Shock:** Ross highlights a massive tactical insight: by putting open-ended self-attribution fields (e.g., *"How did you hear about us?"*) on contact forms, his agency discovered that while only 5% of direct traffic was tracked from ChatGPT, **40% of their actual leads self-attributed to LLMs** [[13:44](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D824&authuser=1), [14:01](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D841&authuser=1)].

### [00:15:38 - 00:23:41] Who Actually Drives SEO Outcomes?

**The Career Risk of Isolation:** Tom drops a contrarian point: in both traditional SEO and the new GEO landscape, the people who actually move the needle are usually **the brand, product, PR, and editorial teams—not the SEO team** [[15:53](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D953&authuser=1), [16:10](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D970&authuser=1)]. SEO teams often own the reporting, but they rarely control the underlying product quality, brand mentions, or public reputation that fuels search engines and LLMs [[16:45](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1005&authuser=1), [17:12](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1032&authuser=1)].

**The Path to the C-Suite:** Tom notes that historically, very few SEO professionals advance to the CMO level because the discipline lacks a clear executive pathway [[17:24](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1044&authuser=1), [18:08](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1088&authuser=1)]. The massive executive interest in AI search right now is a golden opportunity for SEOs to step up, own cross-functional strategies, and bridge this gap [[18:21](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1101&authuser=1), [18:39](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1119&authuser=1)].

**The Bifurcation of Industries:** AI search is splitting into radically different games depending on your vertical [[19:17](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1157&authuser=1)]. For example, B2B companies are thriving on high-intent leads from LLM tools [[20:41](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1241&authuser=1)]. Publishers, however, don't care about citations because citations don't pay for ads or subscriptions [[20:04](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1204&authuser=1), [20:17](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1217&authuser=1)]. For publishing, the AI conversation is entirely about blocking crawlers and securing lucrative data licensing deals [[20:22](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1222&authuser=1), [20:30](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1230&authuser=1)].

### [00:23:42 - 00:29:16] The Future of Technical SEO: "Agentic RAG"

**Beyond Traditional Auditing:** Tom admits he initially thought technical SEO might become less relevant in an AI world [[23:42](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1422&authuser=1)]. However, a recent piece by Mike King on "Agentic RAG" shifted his perspective [[23:56](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1436&authuser=1)].

**Model Distillation:** Modern technical SEOs will likely use model distillation (leveraging open weights or smaller models like Google's Gemma family) to reverse-engineer exactly what vector attributes and weights an LLM associates with their brand versus a competitor [[24:17](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1457&authuser=1), [24:35](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1475&authuser=1)].

**Complex Content Supply Chains:** Indexation is getting increasingly convoluted [[25:53](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1553&authuser=1)]. Instead of just tracking Googlebot, technical SEOs must map how crawlers from OpenAI, Anthropic, or middle-layer search index providers (like Exa or Perplexica) pull data across real-time and training sets [[26:05](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1565&authuser=1), [26:35](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1595&authuser=1)].

### [00:29:17 - 00:37:13] Keeping the Finger on the Pulse of Consumer Behavior

**Triangulating the Data:** Tom emphasizes that nobody has completely "figured out" GEO yet, making it a level playing field [[30:29](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1829&authuser=1), [30:49](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1849&authuser=1)]. Rather than obsessing purely over tactics, the most valuable thing an analyst can track is **how consumer behavior is changing** [[30:59](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1859&authuser=1), [31:04](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1864&authuser=1)].

**Informing Executive Decks:** Senior leadership needs high-level data points to anchor their strategic bets [[31:54](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1914&authuser=1), [32:01](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1921&authuser=1)]. Tom points out that tracking macro trends—like ChatGPT's active user growth or Google's recent I/O announcements regarding AI mode usage—allows you to confidently guide corporate investments [[31:32](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1892&authuser=1), [33:14](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D1994&authuser=1)].

**The Power of Small Data:** Ross mentions tracking a distinct shift where their lead forms showed an influx of "Claude" references over ChatGPT, prompting them to move their entire internal team over to Claude [[34:04](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2044&authuser=1), [34:21](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2061&authuser=1)]. Tom points out that "small data"—like a sharp, crystal-clear shift in a single customer pipeline—influences executive action far faster than massive, abstract spreadsheets [[34:59](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2099&authuser=1), [35:10](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2110&authuser=1), [36:13](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2173&authuser=1)].

### [00:37:14 - 00:44:59] Off-Site Levers & The Trust Problem

**Where the Levers Live:** Ross discusses how his agency builds its strategy around influencing the external corpus of information that feeds LLMs, focusing heavily on industry-specific trust hubs like G2, Google Reviews, Reddit, and key affiliate media [[37:38](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2258&authuser=1), [38:01](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2281&authuser=1)].

**Traffic as a Proxy for Trust:** Tom brings up a fundamental question: out of millions of web pages or Reddit threads, how do LLMs determine which ones to trust? [[42:52](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2572&authuser=1)] He highlights that Google's DOJ antitrust documents proved the massive role "Nav Boost" (user click logs over a trailing 18-month period) plays in search quality [[41:27](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2487&authuser=1), [41:46](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2506&authuser=1)].

He predicts that major LLM platforms will inevitably follow suit—either by utilizing their own user interaction logs or licensing browser clickstream data—to use real-world web traffic as a key filter for model trust [[43:05](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2585&authuser=1), [43:55](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2635&authuser=1)].

### [00:45:00 - 00:54:59] Overhauling the Link UX & Hyper-Personalization

**Moving Beyond the Citation:** Tom calls the current layout of AI citations "naive" [[47:14](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2834&authuser=1), [47:20](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2840&authuser=1)]. Because the LLM gives you the fact directly in the text, users only click a citation about 0.01% of the time to fact-check it [[47:32](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2852&authuser=1), [47:39](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2859&authuser=1)]. He notes that platforms will soon realize that to build sustainable ad models and preserve the open web, they must pivot to a "what to read or do next" UX model that naturally pulls users further down the purchase funnel [[47:44](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2864&authuser=1), [48:03](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D2883&authuser=1)].

**The Shift to "Push" Content:** Tom views it as a limitation that users still have to open an app and manually type prompts [[50:18](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3018&authuser=1), [51:00](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3060&authuser=1)]. Given how much memory these models hold, the future lies in proactive recommendations (similar to the massive traffic numbers driven by Google Discover) [[51:13](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3073&authuser=1), [51:17](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3077&authuser=1)].

**Creepy vs. Useful Personalization:** Tom mentions a study exploring how private brand mentions in a user's Gmail affect their personalized AI search results [[52:08](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3128&authuser=1), [52:20](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3140&authuser=1)]. He shares a personal example where he tested a search for an air purifier in Google's AI mode; the model automatically cross-referenced his personal account history to explicitly calculate recommendations based on the exact square footage and split-level layout of his New York house [[52:30](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3150&authuser=1), [52:46](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3166&authuser=1)]. He emphasizes that consumers gladly trade privacy boundaries once the sheer utility of the tool becomes undeniable [[53:54](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3234&authuser=1), [54:11](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3251&authuser=1)].

### [00:55:00 - 01:00:23] Final Strategy for SEO Teams

**The Ultimate Goal:** At the end of the day, business investments come down to shifting brand preference at the point of sale [[55:43](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3343&authuser=1), [55:48](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3348&authuser=1)].

**Reporting Cadence:** Tom advises SEO and digital teams to report on macro industry shifts and consumer behaviors at the exact same cadence they report on traditional traffic (typically monthly or quarterly) [[57:50](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3470&authuser=1), [57:56](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3476&authuser=1)]. Because AI search disruption is a core C-suite focus, digital professionals should overcommunicate news updates, model changes, and tactical discoveries to stay ahead of the curve [[59:11](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3551&authuser=1), [59:26](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v%3DMTBIMRF1DLE%26t%3D3566&authuser=1)].
